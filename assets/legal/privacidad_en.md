# Privacy Policy

## 1. Controller and contact

Nexo is developed by Auralix Studio ("the Developer"), who also handles questions about the processing of personal data.

- Email: alessandro@aurx.pro
- WhatsApp: +51 907 924 307 (Support section of the app)

## 2. Legal framework

This Policy is governed by Peru's **Law No. 29733 on Personal Data Protection** and its Regulation, approved by **Supreme Decree No. 016-2024-JUS**, as well as other applicable Peruvian rules. The terms "personal data", "processing" and "data subject" have the meaning given by those rules.

## 3. General principle: your data stays on your device

Nexo has no servers of its own that receive, store or analyze your credentials or academic information. The app communicates directly from your device with UPLA's services, just like the official portal, and keeps a local copy so it can work offline.

## 4. Data processed

**a) Credentials and session.** User code, password, and session tokens and cookies issued by UPLA's services.

**b) The user's academic and administrative information.** Profile data (names, code, program or faculty, institutional photo), schedule, grades, attendance, terms, credits, language courses, installments and payments, as made available by UPLA for each account.

**c) Student data in the teacher module.** If the user is a teacher, Nexo shows the data SIGMA makes available to them about the students in their sections: names, code, institutional photo, grades and attendance.

**d) Preferences and local usage.** Theme, language, time format, notification and home settings, attendance drafts and dismissed notices.

**e) Technical connection data.** When communicating with UPLA's services or GitHub, those services receive, as in any internet connection, the device's IP address, the app version and equivalent technical data.

Nexo does not request or process additional sensitive data, does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and does not build profiles for commercial purposes.

## 5. Purposes

Data is processed only to:

- Sign in and keep your session open in UPLA's services.
- Show your academic and administrative information in an organized way, also offline.
- Schedule local reminders and notifications for classes and payments on your device, and show home screen widgets.
- In the teacher module, let teachers view and record attendance and grades for their sections.
- Check whether a new version of the app exists.
- Answer your questions when you contact support.

## 6. Basis for processing

Processing is carried out with your consent, given when you accept this Policy, and because it is necessary to provide the service you request. In the teacher module, student data is processed within the academic relationship the university already enables for the teacher in SIGMA, and for the same purposes.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by signing out and no longer using the app.

## 7. Recipients and transfers

Nexo does not sell, rent or hand over your personal data, does not show advertising, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking tools. Only the following are involved:

- **UPLA's services** (sigma.upla.edu.pe, intranet.upla.edu.pe, services.upla.edu.pe, apidiomas.upla.edu.pe and academico.upla.edu.pe): they receive your credentials and queries in order to return your own information. Their processing is governed by the university's policies.
- **GitHub** (a Microsoft Corporation service, with servers outside Peru): receives a technical request to check whether a new version exists. No academic information or credentials are sent to it. This request may involve a cross-border flow of technical connection data.
- **WhatsApp or your email provider**, only if you decide to contact support, and only with the information you send.

## 8. Storage and retention

- Credentials and session data are stored in the operating system's secure storage (Keychain, Keystore or the Windows equivalent). In the web version, the session is kept in memory only.
- Academic information is stored in a local database on the device, with no encryption beyond what the operating system provides.
- Attendance drafts are kept for at most two days or until they are recorded.
- **Signing out deletes** credentials, the session, cached information and drafts. Uninstalling the app deletes all its local data.

## 9. Security

Communication with UPLA's services and GitHub uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Nexo applies reasonable technical measures to protect data on the device, but its security also depends on screen lock, system updates and the user's care. No measure is infallible.

## 10. Data subject rights

Under Law No. 29733, you may exercise your rights of information, access, rectification, cancellation and objection, and any others recognized by applicable rules.

- Since your data is stored on your device, you can view it in the app, update it in UPLA's systems, and delete it by signing out or uninstalling Nexo.
- For any request or question, write to alessandro@aurx.pro. Requests will be answered within the legal deadlines.
- If you believe your rights have not been addressed, you may file a complaint with Peru's **National Personal Data Protection Authority** at the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights.

Academic information (for example, a grade) can only be corrected by the university in its official systems.

## 11. Minors

Nexo is intended for university students and teachers. Processing of minors' data is subject to the special rules of Law No. 29733 and its Regulation; minors under fourteen must not use the app without their parents' or guardians' authorization.

## 12. Teacher's duty of confidentiality

Teachers who access student data through Nexo must keep it confidential and use it only for academic purposes, under article 17 of Law No. 29733. Capturing, disclosing or sharing that data with third parties is forbidden.

## 13. Changes to this Policy

If the way data is processed changes in a relevant way, this Policy will be updated with its new version and date, and the app will ask for acceptance again before continuing.
