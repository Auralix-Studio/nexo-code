# Cookie and Similar Technologies Policy

## 1. What they are

Cookies are small files a website stores in the browser to remember information between visits. Apps use similar technologies, such as the device's local storage. This Policy explains which ones Nexo uses, what for and how to delete them, complementing the Privacy Policy and in accordance with Law No. 29733 and its Regulation.

## 2. What Nexo does not use

Nexo **does not use** cookies or technologies for advertising, analytics, audience measurement, profiling or tracking, its own or third-party. It does not share device identifiers with ad networks.

## 3. What Nexo does use: strictly necessary items

**a) University sessions, on the server.** At sign-in, the Nexo API receives from UPLA the Intranet session cookies and the SIGMA and Language Center tokens. They are stored **encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on the Nexo server**, not on your device, for the sole purpose of answering your requests. They are deleted when you sign out or when the university expires them.

**b) Device secure storage.** Keeps your credentials and the Nexo API access tokens while the session is open, to sign in again when the session expires. It is deleted when you sign out.

**c) Local preferences storage.** Remembers the theme, language, time format, notification and startup settings, and which version of these documents you accepted. It stays until you change the preference or uninstall the app; signing out does not delete it.

**d) Local information cache.** A copy of your schedule, grades, payments and, for teachers, their section lists, so the app works offline. It is deleted when you sign out.

**e) Drafts and notices.** What was marked while taking attendance before recording it (at most two days) and the classes the teacher marked as not held. They are deleted when recorded, when they expire or when you sign out.

## 4. Web version

In the web version, Nexo uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) for the preferences and cache described, and keeps the session only in memory, so it closes when the page reloads. The Nexo API does not set cookies in your browser.

## 5. Third-party services

The UPLA services, GitHub and WhatsApp may use their own cookies when you visit them from a browser or an app, and third-party apps you connect through "Connect with Nexo" or the calendar are governed by their own policies, not by this one.

## 6. Consent

Since Nexo only uses items strictly necessary to provide the service you request, it does not show a cookie consent notice. If non-essential cookies or technologies are added in the future, your prior, free and informed consent will be requested, and you will be able to refuse them without losing access to the basic features.

## 7. How to delete them

- Sign out from Profile: the university sessions on the server and, on the device, the credentials, cache and drafts are deleted.
- Clear the app's data from the system settings or uninstall it.
- In the web version, clear the site data from your browser settings.
