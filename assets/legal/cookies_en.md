# Cookies and Similar Technologies Policy

## 1. What they are

Cookies are small files a website stores in the browser to remember information between visits. Apps use similar technologies, such as the device's local storage. This Policy explains which ones Nexo uses, what for and how to delete them, as a complement to the Privacy Policy and under Peru's Law No. 29733 and its Regulation.

## 2. What Nexo does not use

Nexo **does not use** cookies or technologies for advertising, analytics, audience measurement, profiling or tracking, whether its own or third-party. It does not share device identifiers with advertising networks.

## 3. What Nexo does use: strictly necessary items

**a) UPLA Intranet session cookies.** When you sign in, the university's Intranet issues session cookies. Nexo stores them in the device's secure storage for the sole purpose of keeping your session open. They are deleted when you sign out or when the university expires them.

**b) Secure storage.** Keeps your credentials and session tokens while the session is open. It is deleted when you sign out.

**c) Local preference storage.** Remembers the theme, language, time format, notification and home settings, and whether you already accepted these documents. It remains until you change the preference or uninstall the app; signing out does not delete it.

**d) Local information cache.** A copy of your schedule, grades, payments and, for teachers, their section lists, so the app works offline. It is deleted when you sign out.

**e) Drafts and notices.** What was marked while taking attendance before recording it (for at most two days) and the classes the teacher indicated were not held. They are deleted when recorded, when they expire, or when you sign out.

## 4. Web version

In the web version, Nexo uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) for the preferences and cache described, and keeps the session in memory only, so it ends when the page is reloaded. Nexo does not set its own cookies for purposes other than running the service.

## 5. Third-party services

UPLA's services, GitHub and WhatsApp may use their own cookies when you visit them from a browser or app. That use is governed by each of their policies, not by this one.

## 6. Consent

Since Nexo only uses items that are strictly necessary to provide the service you request, it does not show a cookie consent notice. If non-essential cookies or technologies are added in the future, your prior, free and informed consent will be requested, and you will be able to reject them without losing access to the basic features.

## 7. How to delete them

- Sign out from Profile: the session, credentials, cache and drafts are deleted.
- Clear the app's data from the system settings, or uninstall it.
- In the web version, clear the site's data from your browser settings.
